| TP-017 | 2026-09-23 | Partners rotate every set: a group of 4 plays 3 sets and everyone partners everyone once. Players sorted by Elo desc (p0≥p1≥p2≥p3): set 1 p0+p3 vs p1+p2, set 2 p0+p2 vs p1+p3, set 3 p0+p1 vs p2+p3. `matchGroups` now stores `players[4]` + `setLineups[3]` + `sets[3]` (scores only); `team1`/`team2`/`matchNumber` removed | Teams no longer need balancing, only the group's Elo spread does; scores stay separate from lineups so players can only edit scores; a group can mix one player's first match with another's second, so per-group `matchNumber` is meaningless. Rotation order proposed by Claude, accepted by Tom |
| TP-018 | 2026-09-23 | The "max−min Elo ≤ 75" rule is no longer a hard limit. The engine always builds the tightest groups it can; a per-season `pairingFlagThreshold` (default 100) flags groups for admin review | Simulated against real Season 3 starting Elos (35 players, 1393–1624): even perfect sorted-Elo chunking leaves a group over 75 in 61% / 54% / 49% of weeks (16 / 20 / 24 available), over 100 in 16% / 6% / 2%, over 125 in 0%; overages come mostly from the top of the ladder. Season 2 spreads grew from 175 to ~600, so a fixed absolute limit drifts. See TP-022 for how the threshold also limits Phase 2 mixing |
| TP-019 | 2026-09-23 | Weeks that aren't a multiple of 4 are filled with `canPlayTwo` volunteers first; players are benched only if there aren't enough volunteers. With more than 24 available (6 courts × 4), the engine fills the courts and lists the extras as `overflow` for the admin (latest responders provisionally left out) — it never silently drops anyone | Keeps as many people playing as possible; the admin, not the algorithm, decides who sits out |
| TP-020 | 2026-09-23 | Pairings land as an admin-only draft: new week status `pairing_draft` between `availability_open` and `matches_set`. Admin publishes by moving the week to `matches_set`. Full order: draft → availability_open → pairing_draft → matches_set → in_progress → complete | Lets the admin review flags, overflow and unmet preferences before players see anything; rules hide non-published groups from non-admins |
| TP-021 | 2026-09-23 | Time-slot rotation: Elo decides who is in a group, slot history decides when each group plays. Blocked slots are hard constraints, preferred slots are soft. Slot history is derived from prior published weeks' `matchGroups` (not stored as a field). Slot assignment enumerates every valid group→slot arrangement. Fairness swaps between groups are limited by a per-season `slotFairnessTolerance` (default 25 Elo points on max spread and on total spread) | Tom's requirement that players rotate slots without extreme Elo mismatches; past seasons had "always early / always late" complaints |
| TP-022 | 2026-09-23 | Keep mixing up who plays together (repeat-grouping penalty in the fairness swaps) "as long as the spread isn't extreme", defined as: Phase 2 swaps can never create a group above the flag threshold (`maxSpread ≤ max(phase1MaxSpread, flagThreshold)`). Refines TP-018: the threshold never affects who is placed or the Phase 1 grouping; it limits Phase 2 mixing and flags groups | Proposed by Claude, accepted by Tom. In the emulator run this allowed the top group to widen from 70.7 to 90.6 to break up a foursome that had played together both history weeks — legal under a threshold of 100 |
| TP-023 | 2026-09-23 | A preferred slot costs a constant bonus (`PREFERRED_SLOT_BONUS = -1`) instead of 0. A preference can still lose when honouring it costs groupmates more rotation; that is accepted as correct. Unmet preferences will be surfaced on the admin draft screen rather than strengthening the weight | At cost 0 a preferred slot tied with unplayed slots and slot order decided, so the preference did nothing. In the seed data the top four players' rotation cost (3 × 2) outweighs one player's −1, which Tom accepted |
| TP-024 | 2026-09-23 | Weekly dinner and golf-sim plans: each week a player answers dinner (none / before my match / after my match) and golf sim (none / before / after). Answers are relative to the player's match and converted to clock times after pairings publish; for 2-match players, before = before the first match, after = after the second. Editable until the week is `complete` | Tom's feature request; clock times depend on the published slot |
